Rapid catastrophe flood response

Business problem

Approach

Deploy NFIP-certified adjusters immediately, utilize the Swarnasky Technologies platform for real-time tracking, and run all estimates through a multi-point quality audit.

Solution

A coordinated flood adjusting model combining certified field adjusters, estimate auditing, and compliance checks.

Designed to process high-volume flood claims with zero compliance errors and reduce claim cycle times by 30%.
