Overview

AI-driven claims and operational intelligence.

We read your policy documents and claim submissions, pull out the details that matter, and sort each claim automatically. Routine claims move straight to a decision. Anything unusual gets flagged so your adjusters spend their time on the cases that need real judgment.

What You Get

Automated Document Extraction

Uses advanced OCR and deep learning models to extract structured data from medical bills, repair estimates, and police reports, eliminating manual data entry.

Automated Triaging & Routing

Scores claims by complexity and severity, instantly fast-tracking low-risk claims for automatic payment while routing complex cases to the right specialized adjuster.

Advanced Fraud & Anomaly Detection

Analyzes claims in real-time against historical patterns, highlighting billing duplicates, inflated estimates, and suspicious activity before payouts occur.

Adjuster Copilot Dashboard

Empowers claims adjusters with AI-generated summaries, policy clause matches, liability scoring, and key document highlights inside a unified portal.

01

Business Problem

What's slowing you down

Your team reviews every medical record, repair estimate, and damage photo by hand. Queues build up, costs climb, payouts run late, and your customers feel it.

02

Swarnasky Solution

How we solve it

We connect to your existing claims systems and let them keep running. Each claim comes in, we structure the evidence, check coverage, and route it in seconds. Simple claims get fast-tracked; complex ones reach an adjuster with a ready-made summary.

03

Expected Result

What you gain

Achieve up to a 70% reduction in cycle times, minimize manual review effort by 40%, ensure 99% data extraction accuracy, and flag potential fraud anomalies before payout.
